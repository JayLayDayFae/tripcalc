While you wait

  • Don't take more just because you don't feel effects yet. Edibles can be delayed and may build for several hours.
  • Avoid mixing substances, including alcohol, THC, or mushroom products.
  • Stay somewhere comfortable with a trusted sober person, sip water normally, and don't drive or cycle in traffic.
  • Unregulated mushroom products can contain unexpected ingredients. A milligram number alone does not verify what's inside.

Get urgent medical help for trouble breathing, seizures, collapse, severe chest pain, or if someone is difficult to wake. In the U.S., Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222.

Trip Calc provides broad educational ranges, not a prediction of your personal experience or a dosing recommendation. Onset and duration vary with product, route, food, metabolism, and other factors. Amount, height, weight, and tolerance cannot reliably calculate when a substance will kick in.
